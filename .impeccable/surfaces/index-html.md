## Direction contract

THESIS: The checklist is a levelling run, not a form. Daily, 4H and 15m are three legs measured off a benchmark, and the status bar tells you whether the run closes. Refuses the dark-fintech dashboard with a glowing accent.
OWN-WORLD: Surveyor's level book. Cool green-grey sheet on a darker ground, blue-green rule lines, graphite ink, survey-stake yellow as the single accent, bull green / bear red kept as semantics. Barlow Condensed lettering, Public Sans reading text, Spline Sans Mono for every measurement. 4px corners, hairline borders, no shadows. Dark mode is the same book read under a headlamp.
STORY: A solo trader opens it before an entry, ticks required readings, sees "Setup valid" or "No trade" at a glance, logs the result, and later sees which readings actually predicted wins.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand bar, title and one-line lede, then the sticky run bar: bias toggle left, verdict right, below it a staff of discrete cells (one per required item, grouped 1D / 4H / 15m / Risk) that fill yellow and close to green. First legs of the checklist begin underneath. Bottom tab bar: Check, Log, Stats, Risk.
